About me

Alongside narrative filmmaking, I work across branded content, editing, and creative direction, developing projects that range from short films and experimental media to social campaigns and interactive storytelling experiences. I'm particularly drawn to horror and psychological storytelling, using texture, sound, pacing, and visual symbolism to create lingering emotional impact.

Sheep in Wolf's Clothing

Description

Sheep in Wolf’s Clothing is a film about survival.

Four strangers are trapped in a room due to the outbreak of an unknown disease. When food starts running low, what will be sacrificed.

Four strangers, Tamasvi, Noor, Dabria and Selma have been locked in a makeshift bunker for weeks. The outside situation is dire and when the radio tells them rescue teams will not be arriving, they start losing hope. Noor makes a joke about sacrificing one of them for food, and the next day one goes missing.

This is a story about the struggle between morality and survival and what is left when difficult choices must be made.

Psychosis

Description

Psychosis is a film about surviving mania.

A doctor investigating an unknown strain of a virus accidentally gets infected with it. She’s unaware of her worsening condition as she carries on her research.

Dr. Mallory receives a patient with an unknown virus, the story unfolds in the form of digital reports she makes..
